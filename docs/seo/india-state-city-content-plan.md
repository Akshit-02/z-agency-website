# India State & City Content Plan

Started 2026-10-04. Companion to [india-state-city-keyword-map.md](india-state-city-keyword-map.md), [india-state-city-publishing-log.md](india-state-city-publishing-log.md) and [india-local-seo-implementation-report.md](india-local-seo-implementation-report.md).

## Principles

1. **One article per distinct opportunity, not per city × service.** A location article is only created when (a) the location is searched with that service, (b) at least four relevant companies with a confirmed local office can be verified, and (c) there is genuinely different local context to write about.
2. **ZSpace Labs has no office anywhere.** Every location article says ZSpace works with businesses there remotely. No local clients, projects or experience are implied.
3. **No local statistics without a source.** Local context is limited to widely documented facts (e.g. Mumbai hosts RBI, BSE and NSE; Surat's textile and diamond trades), with no market sizes or growth figures.
4. **Shortlist format.** Location articles use the same disclosed-shortlist template as the India-wide articles (ZSpace first as publisher, four verified local companies, criteria, tables, questions, FAQs, sources), plus location-specific sections.
5. **Small batches.** Batch 1 is five articles. The next batch should wait for Search Console data on batch 1 (see Publishing log).

## Rules for every future article

Before writing: confirm four relevant companies with a local office on official sites; confirm the topic isn't covered by an existing article; record the research in this file.
Before publishing: unique title and description; location-specific sections that would not make sense with another city's name swapped in; remote-only note for ZSpace; all links working; build passes.
