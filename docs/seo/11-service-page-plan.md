# 11. Service-page improvement plan

The six service pages already meet 12 of the brief's 15 points. The missing three are proof, honest cost context and (until batch 1) international delivery. Proof and cost need owner input; nothing should be invented to fill them.

| # | Brief requirement | Status |

## Prioritised plan

| Priority | Change | Pages | Needs | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Add 1 case study per service when real projects are approved (problem, what was built, measured outcome only if measured) | All | Owner: projects + permission | Low |
| P1 | Add a "What it typically costs" block with the bands ZSpace actually quotes, in INR and USD (and the currency each market is invoiced in) | All | Owner: price bands | Low |

| P3 | Separate pages for "web application development" or "ecommerce development" | — | Only if Search Console shows distinct demand the current pages don't serve | Medium (cannibalisation) |

## Not recommended

- Country-specific copies of service pages (`/uk/services/...`). See `12-international-landing-pages.md`.
- "Leading", "best" or guaranteed-results wording.
- Review or rating schema until there are real, visible reviews.
