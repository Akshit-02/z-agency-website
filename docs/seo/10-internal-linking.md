# 10. Internal linking recommendations

Articles link up to services well (all 1,057 link to contact with a source parameter; 889 link to two or more service pages within the article area). Services and industry pages link down weakly: 6 articles per service page, and before batch 1, 1–2 generic articles per industry page.

## Structure

1. **Service pages (6)**: the conversion targets. Link to 3–4 pillar guides, the cost guide, the "how to choose" guide, up to 5 industries, and contact with `?src=`.
2. **Industry pages (12)**: link to 4–6 industry-matched articles and 2–3 services. **Done in batch 1** (automotive 3 and education 2, because those clusters are thin).
3. **Pillar and hub guides (48)**: linked from services and category pages; each should link to its service and 8–12 cluster articles.
4. **Cluster articles (~1,000)**: link to their pillar, 2–3 siblings and the service in body copy, with descriptive anchors.
5. **Case studies (future)**: linked from the matching service and industry pages.
6. **Market pages (future, conditional)**: linked from the "Where we work" section on service pages and from the matching country articles.
