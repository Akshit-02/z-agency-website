## Facts and verification status

### Checked on the source page

**Abu Dhabi economy and ecosystem**
- SCAD Q3 2025 GDP (+7.7%, AED 325.7bn, 54% non-oil)
- ADRA 2025 licence figures: new licences +29%; Tajer licences 7,187 → 8,901
- ADGM H1 2026: 13,974 active licences; 3,986 operational entities
- Hub71: 390 startups

**Government digital**
- DGE Digital Strategy: AED 13bn; AI-native government by 2027; 100% sovereign cloud
- TAMM H1 2026: 55.5m transactions, 98% digital (Gulf News)

**Healthcare**
- ADHICS V2 (effective Aug 2024), including the CS 1.2 cloud control
- ADHICS FAQ v1.3: small clinics must comply; Malaffi prerequisite

**Federal and platforms**
- UAE PASS docs: private-sector eligibility, trade licence, phases
- u.ae: PDPL, consumer protection, WCAG
- Shopify Payments UAE requirements
- Google: hreflang, Core Web Vitals, Business Profile service areas
- Azure and Oracle regions
- Microsoft AI diffusion; AWS/UAE AI Office

### Seen in search results only

- ADGM DPR consent rules: the guidance PDF returned 403. The article states only "clear affirmative act" and "pre-ticked boxes do not count".
- Shopify Horizon RTL support.
- Tajer Abu Dhabi licence scope.

### Excluded

- A supposed "Abu Dhabi Law No. 3 of 2023" on Arabic, which appears not to exist.
- Any website Arabic mandate.
- SME share figures, which have conflicting versions.
- Current ADIO programme terms.
- Abu Dhabi government vendor security standards (ADISS status unverified).
