## Market detail

### India
- **Buyer language** [Inference from SERPs and the site]: "company" for web and app development, "agency" for Shopify, UI/UX and CRO; budgets in INR (lakhs); WhatsApp enquiries; UPI and cash on delivery; GST invoicing.
- **Pricing context**: the contact form asks for budgets in USD only. India visitors need INR bands (owner to set).
- **Opportunities**: DPDP-ready forms, consent and analytics (implementation, not legal advice); Shopify for D2C (COD and RTO, pin-code delivery promises, app bloat); AI automation that connects WhatsApp, spreadsheets and CRMs.

### UAE
- **Requirements**: Arabic and right-to-left layouts; PDPL federally, plus DIFC and ADGM regimes in those free zones; e-invoicing from 2027.
- **Payments** [Inference, verify per provider before publishing]: cards and Apple Pay, BNPL providers such as Tabby and Tamara, and cash on delivery still common in ecommerce.
- **Pricing context**: AED. Local studios publish ranges from roughly AED 5k to 100k+ [Vendor].
- **Opportunities**: ERP-to-ASP e-invoicing integration and invoice data quality (never tax advice or ASP status); bilingual Shopify stores.

### Australia
- **Spelling and terms**: Australian English (organisation, optimisation), GST, ABN, "eCommerce" common in Australia Post materials.
- **Requirements**: Privacy Act 1988 and the APPs; ADM disclosure from 10 Dec 2026; Disability Discrimination Act 1992 for accessibility; Australian Consumer Law guarantees.
- **Payments** [Inference, verify]: cards, PayID/PayTo on the NPP, Afterpay and other BNPL.
- **Pricing context**: AUD. Offshore benchmarks cited by vendors: AUD 30–80/hour [Vendor].
- **Opportunities**: mapping which software informs decisions for ADM disclosures (for the client's lawyers to finalise); integrations with Xero, MYOB, Australia Post and Peppol (verified in `api-integration-australia`).

### United Kingdom
- **Spelling and terms**: British English; "VAT"; "agency" for most digital services.
- **Requirements**: UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; PECR and the ICO's 2026 storage and access guidance; Equality Act 2010 (WCAG 2.2 AA as the usual benchmark).
- **Payments** [Inference, verify]: cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, Klarna and Clearpay for BNPL.
- **Pricing context**: GBP. London Shopify Plus agencies quote £50k–150k builds and publish minimums [Vendor].
- **Opportunities**: Shopify and web builds for brands that want senior remote teams at lower cost than London agencies, if delivery terms and references can be shown.

### Singapore
- **Requirements**: PDPA 2012 ([PDPC](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act)); InvoiceNow (Peppol) for e-invoicing [Vendor, verify with IMDA].
- **Payments** [Inference, verify]: cards, PayNow, GrabPay.
- **Pricing context**: SGD. Vendor guides put a 5–10 page brochure site at S$4.5k–14k [Vendor].
- **Constraint**: grant-funded projects require pre-approved local vendors and 30% local equity [Official].
