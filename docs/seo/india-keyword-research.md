# India Keyword Research

Researched 2026-10-04 for ZSpace Labs' India-wide commercial strategy.

## Method and limitations

- Live web searches were run for the head term in each service category plus a CRO-specific variant. The search tool available returns **US-localised results**, not google.co.in results, so the SERP observations below describe which pages rank internationally for India-modified queries. They are a strong but imperfect proxy for Indian results and should be re-checked from an Indian IP or in Google Search Console once the pages are indexed.
- **No search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPC or traffic estimates are claimed.** No keyword platform (Ahrefs, Semrush, DataForSEO, Google Keyword Planner) was connected. Where this document ranks priorities, it does so on intent and commercial value, which is marked as editorial judgement.
- Competitor facts are recorded separately in [india-competitor-database.md](india-competitor-database.md).

## What the results pages look like (all six categories)

The same four page types dominate every "best X agency/company in India" query:

1. **Directories and review platforms**: Clutch (`clutch.co/web-developers/india`, `clutch.co/in/agencies/ui-ux`), DesignRush (`designrush.com/agency/.../in`, often many paginated pages), GoodFirms. They rank because of authority and breadth, and they sell placement.
2. **Agency-published listicles**, usually with the publisher in or near the top: Octet's "11 Best Design Agencies in India", ProCreator's "Top UI UX design agency in India", upGrowth's "CRO agencies India", ROI Minds' "Top conversion rate optimization agencies in India", Debut Infotech's "App development companies in India", Squareboat's "Web development company in India", Aureate Labs' "Top Shopify web design agency picks". **This confirms that self-published comparison articles are an established, competitive format for these queries**, and that transparent disclosure is a differentiator because most do not disclose the publisher's interest clearly.
3. **Contributed posts on publishing platforms**: YourStory "MyStory" posts, Medium posts. Low editorial quality, but they rank on domain authority.
4. **SaaS vendor content**: GemPages (Shopify page builder) "Top CRO agencies in India", Fibr and Mida (CRO tools) "Best CRO agency in India" listings.

Implication: ZSpace Labs can't outrank directories on authority in the short term. The realistic route is (a) strong, specific service pages that match "company in India" intent, (b) genuinely useful, disclosed comparison articles that beat thin listicles on depth, and (c) earning listings and mentions on the same directories (see the authority plan in [india-ranking-strategy.md](india-ranking-strategy.md)).

## Related questions seen across results

- "Which is the best web/app/Shopify company in India?" Every credible answer depends on project type, so the listicles answer directly and then explain fit.
- "How much does it cost to build a website/app in India?" Covered by existing cost articles. India-specific pricing is not published because ZSpace Labs has not published its rates, and inventing market ranges is not acceptable.
- "How do I choose an agency?" / "What should I ask before hiring?" Covered by existing and new how-to-choose guides.
- "Agency vs freelancer?" Covered by existing articles (website-development-company-vs-freelancer, shopify-developer-vs-agency-which-to-hire).

## Commercial modifiers observed

best, top, company, agency, experts, services, developers, custom, Shopify Plus, startup, ecommerce, near me (city variants), cost, price.

## Content gaps found

1. No India-intent service pages: the six service pages had no India targeting in titles, H1s, copy or schema. **Fixed in this project.**
2. No disclosed comparison articles for any category. **Six published in this project.**
3. No "how to choose" guides for AI automation, UI/UX or CRO agencies, and no UI vs UX or CRM automation article. **Five published.**
4. Thin directory and listicle pages rarely explain *fit*: which kind of provider suits which project. ZSpace Labs' articles lead with fit and disclose the publisher's interest, which is the main differentiation available without third-party authority.
