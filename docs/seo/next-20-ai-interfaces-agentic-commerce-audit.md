# Next 20: AI Interfaces and Agentic Commerce — Content Audit

Audited 2026-10-08 against all 822 published articles, dumped from `src/lib/blog-data*.ts` (titles, slugs, excerpts, section headings and full body text). Posts render through `src/app/blogs/[slug]/page.tsx` (canonical, Open Graph, BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage schema from the shared template), sitemap from `src/app/sitemap.ts`, robots from `src/app/robots.ts`.

**Method.** Each proposal was compared by URL, title, H1, section headings and full-text search for its defining terms. Term counts before writing: "generative UI" 0, "MCP Apps" 0, "agentic checkout" 1 (one bullet list in `agentic-commerce`), "delegated payment" 1, "Agentic Commerce Protocol" 2, "Universal Commerce Protocol" 2, "handoff" across many posts (chat-to-human handoff only). No keyword-volume tool is configured, so **no search volumes, difficulty scores or adoption figures are given**. Intent was judged from query wording, current vendor and specification terminology, and the pages that answer each query today.

**Outcome:** 2 GREEN, 11 YELLOW, 7 RED. **15 articles published** (13 proposals, one of which absorbs another, plus 2 replacements). The 7 RED intents are served by extending the existing owner URL.

## Why the RED decisions

- **#2, #8, #11, #13, #18, #19** each restate a page that already ranks for the same intent, several published within the last week. A second URL would split signals and give readers two near-identical pages; extending the owner URL keeps one strong answer.
- **#4** and **#6** asked for the same risk matrix. One article now owns "when to ask" plus the approval-card anatomy; the existing human-in-the-loop article keeps the operational/queue intent.
- No replacements were added to Cluster B: candidate topics either cannibalized the protocol comparison or the checkout article, or lacked verifiable substance. The RED Cluster B intents are covered by the extended owner pages, which the stack hub links to.
