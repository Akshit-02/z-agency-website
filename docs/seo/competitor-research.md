## What competitors do that ZSpace Labs did not

1. **Depth on service pages.** Competitor service pages are typically 2,000–4,500 words with: a definition section ("What is…"), service sub-types, process, tech stack, industries, FAQs (6–15), related blogs, case studies and testimonials. ZSpace Labs' service pages were ~600 words with 3 FAQs. **Implemented:** definition, audience, use cases, process heading, curated guides and 7 FAQs per service (now ~740 words of main content plus structured sections). Case studies and testimonials were **not** added: none exist in the codebase and none may be invented.
2. **Geographic targeting.** Most Indian competitors run "… company in India" and city pages. Not replicated (see keyword-map.md geographic strategy).
3. **Social proof.** Client logos, ratings, review counts and awards are central to every competitor page. ZSpace Labs has none published. This is the largest remaining conversion and authority gap and can only be closed with real evidence.
4. **Pricing signals.** Some competitors publish price ranges or engagement models. ZSpace Labs explains cost drivers in guides; publishing real starting prices or engagement models would help commercial intent if the business is willing.

## Where ZSpace Labs is stronger

- **Educational depth:** 728 articles with direct answers, FAQs, diagrams and references, organised into six topic hubs. Competitor blogs reviewed were typically thinner and more promotional.
- **Schema honesty:** several competitors mark up their own services with AggregateRating, Review and Product/Offer schema, which Google's guidelines do not support for self-serving reviews. ZSpace Labs uses only accurate Organization, WebSite, Service, BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage and FAQPage markup.
- **Unified positioning:** design, engineering, Shopify, AI and CRO from one team, which most competitors split across separate pages without connecting them.

## Opportunities

- Comparison and decision guides competitors rarely publish well: choosing an AI automation agency, choosing a UI/UX agency, choosing a CRO agency, n8n vs Zapier vs Make, WooCommerce to Shopify migration (see content-roadmap.md).
- Cost guides for maintenance, redesigns and audits, written around cost drivers rather than invented price lists.
- Original assets: diagrams already exist on every article; publishing real project write-ups (with permission) would add the evidence competitors use.
