# India State & City Keyword Map

Researched 2026-10-04. **No search volumes, difficulty scores, CPC or positions are claimed.** No keyword platform was available, and the search tool returns US-localised results, so SERP observations are a proxy for google.co.in. Prioritisation is editorial judgement based on intent, service fit and whether a useful, verifiable article can be written.

## SERP observations (location + service queries)

Queries checked: "best web development company in Bengaluru", "best mobile app development company in Delhi NCR Noida Gurugram", "best Shopify development agency Ahmedabad Surat Gujarat", "best UI UX design agency in Mumbai", "AI automation company Ahmedabad AI development", "best web development company in Pune".

- **Directories** (DesignRush, GoodFirms, Clutch) appear for every query, often with many paginated city pages.
- **Agency self-published city lists** are common: ProCreator and Yellow Slice both publish "UI UX design companies in Mumbai" lists; StudioKrew publishes NCR app rankings; Codewave publishes Bengaluru design lists; IRPR publishes Pune lists. Most don't clearly disclose the publisher's interest.
- **City service pages** from agencies ("AI development company in Ahmedabad", "UI UX agency Mumbai") also rank.
- Low-quality contributed posts (forums, document-sharing sites) appear, which suggests the queries aren't saturated with strong editorial content.

Implication: a disclosed, specific, genuinely local shortlist is a credible way to compete, but directories will keep ranking on authority. Earning directory listings remains part of the plan.

## Mapping rules

- **"[service] company in [city]"** (transactional) → the national service page. Service pages state that ZSpace serves businesses across India remotely. City-specific service pages are not created because ZSpace has no local presence (doorway risk).
- **"best [service] company/agency in [city]"** (commercial investigation) → the city shortlist, where one exists; otherwise the India-wide shortlist.
- **"how to choose / cost / vs"** (informational) → existing guides; never duplicated per city.

## Cannibalisation checks

- City shortlists vs India-wide shortlists: different modifiers (city vs country); city articles link up to the India article and vice versa. Different company sets where possible (only GeekyAnts, Aalpha, Appinventiv, SparxIT, OpenXcell, Simform and the Gujarat Shopify agencies repeat, with location-specific framing).
- City shortlists vs service pages: different intent (comparison vs hire). Service pages remain the CTA destination from every article.
- Gujarat (state) vs Ahmedabad (city): different services (Shopify vs AI), so no overlap.
