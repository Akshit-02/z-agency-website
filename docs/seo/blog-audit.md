## How this audit was done

- **Automated checks on all 728 articles:** word count, direct-answer section, FAQs, contextual service links, internal links in and out, external references, title and meta description length, publication dates and schema.
- **Manual editorial review** of the weakest group (the 18 original articles in `blog-data.ts`), the hub articles that needed cluster sections, and the articles touched by date, title and link fixes. The 562 articles written in earlier batches of this engagement were researched, fact-checked and validated at publication time (see earlier batch reports); they were re-checked here with the automated checks only.
- **Overlap analysis:** 5-word-shingle text similarity across all pairs sharing slug terms, and primary-keyword collisions. Only one pair exceeded 4% shingle overlap and only one pair shared a normalised primary keyword; both were differentiated rather than merged.
- **External references:** all 323 unique external URLs cited across articles were checked; all resolve (5 return 403/202 to scripts and were verified manually in a browser or via search).
