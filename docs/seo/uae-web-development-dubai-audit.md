## Search results audit (approximate)

The search tool is US-based. It shows no local pack and no rank positions, so results approximate, not reproduce, Google.ae.

Queries: web development company Dubai, website development company Dubai, web development agency Dubai, web development Dubai, web developers Dubai.

- **ZSpace is absent from all five result sets.** No ZSpace page targeted these terms before this article.
- **Overseas vendors with Dubai city landing pages dominate.** These came from the UK, India and Bangladesh. This is the same remote model as ZSpace, so a differentiated buyer's guide is a better entry point than another city landing page.
- **"Top 10" listicles are mostly published by a vendor that ranks itself first,** with no method stated.
- **Spam and user-generated pages rank:** Gumroad, Substack, forum posts and hijacked domains. That signals weak competition for genuinely useful content.
- **No vendor-neutral buyer's guide was found.** The closest were agency blog posts on choosing a Dubai web company, all promotional.
- **Clutch, GoodFirms and DesignRush did not surface** in these US-based results.

## Content gaps the article fills

1. **Ownership:** IP assignment, repository and account ownership, and the .ae / co.ae rules. Only 3 of 13 firms address it.
2. **Hosting location and data protection:** UAE cloud regions, PDPL, DIFC cookie consent, and health and banking localisation. Only 1 firm addresses it.
3. **Maintenance:** scope, SLA severity levels, and support hours in UAE time.
4. **Proposal comparison:** normalisation table, milestone payments and three-year cost.
5. **Accessibility:** WCAG benchmark and legal status. No firm mentions it.
6. **Arabic/RTL in practice, and bilingual hreflang.**
7. **Core Web Vitals targets** in acceptance criteria, including INP.
8. **UAE payment providers by name,** plus ecommerce law, Arabic invoices and the e-invoicing link.
9. **Remote vendors:** contracting entity, licence, governing law and overlapping hours.
10. **A usable weighted scorecard,** with a worked hypothetical example.

## Facts used and their status

Checked on the official or primary page:
- TDRA .ae policy: .ae is unrestricted; co.ae requires a UAE trade licence or trademark.
- u.ae pages: PDPL; consumer protection, including Arabic invoices and Arabic product information; Decree-Law 14/2023 detailed digital invoices; u.ae WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA.
- DIFC Data Protection Regulations: Reg. 9.
- Cloud regions: AWS me-central-1 (2022), Azure UAE North and UAE Central, Oracle Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Google Cloud has no UAE region.
- Cloudflare: lists a Dubai data centre.
- Core Web Vitals thresholds, and INP replacing FID (March 2024).
- Google: redirects for at least 1 year; hreflang.
- OWASP Top 10:2025.
- PCI DSS v4.0.1, requirements 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 (after 31 March 2025).
- Checkout.com CBUAE licence; Stripe UAE availability.

From law-firm commentary (the official text could not be loaded):
- Copyright Decree-Law 38/2021, Art. 28 (CMS, Gowling WLG).
- Federal Law 2/2019, Art. 13 on health data (Latham & Watkins).
