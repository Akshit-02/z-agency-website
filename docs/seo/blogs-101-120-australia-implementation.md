## Research and verification

The research files are in the session scratchpad (`batch3/research-au-ai.md`, `research-au-commerce.md`, `research-au-tech.md`). Several government sites (ai.gov.au, industry.gov.au, acma.gov.au, ato.gov.au, cyber.gov.au) timed out or returned 403. Facts from those sites are attributed as reported or secondary in the text.

**Verified on primary pages during writing or QA:**
- Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 commencement table (legislation.gov.au): statutory tort from 10 Jun 2025; ADM transparency from 10 Dec 2026.
- OAIC ADM resources release (30 Sep 2026); OAIC AI products guidance; OAIC small business exemption; 2025 NDB statistics.
- NDB 30-day assessment rule (s 26WH(2), OAIC Part 4 guidance).
- RBA surcharging decision (31 Mar 2026; effective 1 Oct 2026; eftpos/Visa/Mastercard only).
- Australia Post eCommerce Report 2026; Google Merchant Center tax-inclusive pricing for Australia; Google AI Mode in Australia (8 Oct 2025); StatCounter Australia (Sep 2026).
- Home Affairs ransomware payment reporting factsheet; NSW daylight saving dates; W3C WCAG 2.2.

**Statistics:**
- Every percentage was cross-checked against the research files.
- Every non-research AUD figure belongs to a labelled illustrative example, and the arithmetic in those examples was checked.
