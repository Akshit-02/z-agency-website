# 07. Competitor and search-intent research

Researched 12 and 17 October 2026. Local SERPs could not be observed (US-based search tool), so the competitors below are "who invests in these queries and markets", **not** verified local rankings. No traffic or ranking figures for competitors are stated.

## Search intent by service (all markets)

| Service | Commercial queries (pattern) | Informational queries ZSpace already covers | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website development | "[web development] company [country/city]", "cost of a website [country]" | Process, timeline, Next.js, headless, redesign vs rebuild | Cost guide gives no figures; no proof |
| Mobile apps | "app development company [country]", "app development cost" | Native vs cross-platform, React Native, maintenance, store fees (sourced) | Thin cluster (54 articles) relative to demand signals |
| AI automation | "AI automation agency", "n8n agency", "AI agent development company" | Workflow design, agents, evaluation, governance (239 articles) | Saturated; differentiation is "pilot to production", not more articles |
| UI/UX | "UI/UX design agency [country]", "UX audit" | Process, heuristics, design systems, audits | Portfolio/case studies |
| Shopify | "Shopify development agency [country]", "Shopify Plus partner", "Shopify store cost" | Themes, CRO, migration, Markets, checkout | Partner status unverified; no store examples |
| CRO | "CRO agency", "conversion rate optimisation audit" | Audits, testing, funnels (108 articles) | Proof of results |

## Competitor patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | What it means for ZSpace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof first | Ranking agencies show case studies, client logos, named teams and directory reviews [Vendor pages] | The largest gap; content volume can't close it |
| Local presence or explicit delivery model | Offshore firms targeting AU and UK publish time-zone overlap, contract jurisdiction, IP ownership and currency ([Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/australia), [Wisemonk](https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/australian-tech-company-offshore-development-team-india)) [Vendor] | Service pages now name five markets; delivery terms still needed |
| Platform credentials | Shopify agencies cite partner tiers, checkable in Shopify's directory | Claim only what is verified |
| Self-ranked listicles | 2026 "best Shopify agency" lists are commonly written by agencies ranking themselves first ([IWD](https://www.iwdagency.com/blogs/news/best-shopify-plus-agencies/)) [Vendor]; analysts link the pattern to visibility losses since Dec 2025 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-cracking-down-self-promotional-best-of-listicles-468227)) [Industry] | Reframe ZSpace's 11 lists (approval Q3) |
| Published pricing | Singapore and UAE studios often publish ranges; London Plus agencies publish minimums or "on request" [Vendor] | Real ZSpace price bands would be a differentiator |
