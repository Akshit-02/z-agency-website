**How to read this file**

- **Cluster** is assigned from the slug; **intent** and **likely primary keyword** are inferred from the title pattern and H1, so treat them as a starting point rather than keyword-tool data (no search-volume or keyword-difficulty source is connected to this project).
- **Quality** flags come from measured word count, internal-link count and FAQ count.
- **Overlap** lists other articles whose title keywords overlap strongly. Many overlaps are intentional hub/spoke pairs (for example an industry-specific variant of a general guide); the cannibalisation section below lists the pairs that genuinely compete.
- Every article already has: one H1, canonical URL, Open Graph image, BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList schema (and FAQPage when FAQs are visible) from the shared template in `src/app/blogs/[slug]/page.tsx`, and a "Quick answer" opening section.
